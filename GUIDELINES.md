# Toulouse DevOps – brand guidelines

These rules mirror <https://toulouse-devops.org/brand/>.

## Logo

The logo combines the DevOps infinity loop with the Occitan cross of
Toulouse, above the name set in Ubuntu Medium.

- **Respect the artwork**: no distortion, no recolouring, no drop shadow, no
  rotation, no added effects.
- **Background**: place the logo on a light background (white, `#fafbfc`, or
  Bleu pâle `#e8eef5`) to keep it legible.
- **Clear space**: keep a free margin around the logo at least equal to the
  height of the Occitan cross. No text or graphic element may enter it.
- **Minimum size**: 32 px high on screen, 10 mm high in print.
- **Symbol only** (`toulouse-devops-mark`): use it when the name "Toulouse
  DevOps" is already written nearby, or for square/small formats (avatar,
  favicon, sticker). The same rules apply.

## Naming

Write **Toulouse DevOps**: capital D and O in "DevOps", with a space between
the two words.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Toulouse DevOps | Toulouse Devops |
| | ToulouseDevOps |
| | TLS DevOps |

## Colours

The two main colours come from the logo: Bleu principal `#0e2d5a` and
Rouge accent `#c83737`. Use the red sparingly, for calls to action and
highlights. On the website, the brighter "Bleu web" `#1a4580` replaces the
logo blue for interface elements; keep `#0e2d5a` for print. The full
palette is in [README.md](README.md#colours) and in
machine-readable form in [`colors/`](colors/).

## Typography

- Ubuntu (Medium / Bold) for the logo and headings.
- Inter for body text.
- JetBrains Mono for code.

## Tone

Professional but relaxed: we talk tech seriously, around a drink and a
pizza. Informal French ("tu") is used in community communication.
